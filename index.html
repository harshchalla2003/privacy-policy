At a glance

This policy explains what Verdelle reads on your phone, what it keeps, and where that information goes. In short: it stays in the app's private storage on your device, and Verdelle sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

  • No accountNothing to sign up for, no email or name asked.
  • No internet accessThe app does not request Android's internet permission.
  • No ads or analyticsNo advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code.
  • Stored on your phoneStep totals and your garden live in private app storage.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect any information from you: Verdelle has no way to send it to us. The app does read and store some information on your phone so it can work. That is described here.

Steps

With your permission, Verdelle reads your phone's built-in hardware step counter. From those readings it works out your steps for each day and your lifetime total. It does not use your location, and it does not detect steps from motion sensors itself.

Screen time by app category Optional

Only if you switch on Digital Balance and turn on Android's Usage access for Verdelle, the app reads Android's record of when apps were open and adds the time up by app category (for example social, video or games), using the category each app's developer declared. The list of apps is used only on your phone, only for that calculation, and is then discarded. App names are not saved and are not shown anywhere in Verdelle.

Device details used for counting

To count correctly across restarts, Verdelle reads your phone's boot count and the time it last started, and stores them with the step readings. It also creates a random identifier, kept in a folder that Android excludes from backups, which is used only on your phone to notice when data has been restored onto a new install.

What Verdelle stores on your phone

  • Your step totals for each day, your lifetime total, and the date counting began
  • The latest step-counter reading, boot count and time, to calculate the next difference
  • Up to 400 step readings taken in the background, until the app next opens
  • Your garden: which plants you have found, and when
  • Digital Balance settings (on or off, your daily intention, the categories you count) and, for each completed day, one total of minutes in those categories alongside the intention you had set
  • Your motion setting and whether you have finished the introduction

How we use information

Everything Verdelle reads is used inside the app, on your phone, for these purposes only:

  • Growing your garden. Your lifetime steps decide what grows; something changes every 500 steps.
  • Showing your walks. Today's steps, the last seven days and your totals.
  • Placing steps on the right day. About once an hour, Android's WorkManager lets Verdelle take one reading of the step counter in the background. It does not run a constant service.
  • Quiet days (optional). After each day ends, the category minutes you chose to count are compared with your own daily intention. A day within it becomes a quiet day.

Verdelle does not use your information for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than the features above.

Data storage and security

Verdelle keeps its information in the app's private storage on your phone, which Android's app sandbox protects from other apps. Because the app has no internet access, nothing is transmitted by Verdelle.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include Verdelle's app data (your step totals, garden, and Digital Balance day totals) in your Google Account backup and when you transfer data to a new phone. This is done by Android and Google under your Google Account settings; Verdelle itself does not send this data, and we cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings. When data is restored to a new phone, Verdelle keeps your garden and history but starts counting steps afresh on that phone.

Third-party services

Verdelle contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. It uses two open-source building blocks that run entirely on your phone and do not transmit data:

  • Android Jetpack WorkManager, which schedules the hourly step reading.
  • Flutter's path_provider, which tells the app where its private storage folder is.

Outside the app itself, two Google services may be involved, each under Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Google Play, which distributes the app. Google Play may give us aggregated statistics, such as install counts and crash rates from users who chose to share usage and diagnostics with Google. These do not identify you and do not include your steps or garden.
  • Android backup, if you have it turned on (see Data storage and security).

Information sharing

We do not sell, share, rent or transfer your information to anyone. Verdelle has no means of sending it, and we never receive it.

Data retention and deletion

Information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • In Verdelle: Settings → Start the garden over… clears your garden, journal, walking history and Digital Balance days.
  • In Android: Settings → Apps → Verdelle → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstalling the app, deletes everything Verdelle stored.
  • Turning off Digital Balance stops any further reading of usage information.

Copies in an Android backup follow your Google Account backup settings. Because we never receive your information, we cannot access, export or delete it for you, and there is no account to delete.

Children's privacy

Verdelle is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, and to object to its use. With Verdelle, you hold all of the information yourself, on your phone, and you can see it in the app and delete it as described above. We do not hold any of your information, so there is nothing for us to provide, correct or erase. If you have a question or request, contact us and we will reply.

Changes to this policy

If Verdelle's handling of information changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that uses the internet, we will update this page and its effective date, and describe the change in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
